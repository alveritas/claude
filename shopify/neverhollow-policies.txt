==================== REFUND POLICY ====================

All sales are final once your order ships. We don’t accept returns or exchanges, so please check the product details before you order, and email us at neverhollowonline@gmail.com if you’re unsure about anything.

Pre-orders
Pre-order pieces are made in one production run after pre-orders close, and each one shows its ship-by date on the product page, in your bag and on your order confirmation. If the production run doesn’t go ahead, we refund your order in full. If your order is going to ship later than its ship-by date, we’ll email you before that date with a new date, and you can choose a full refund instead of waiting.

Damaged or incorrect items
If your order arrives damaged or faulty, or isn’t what you ordered, email us as soon as you can with your order number and photos. We’ll replace the item or refund you in full, including the original shipping.

Your legal rights
This policy doesn’t limit any rights you have under the law where you live. If you’re in the UK, the EU or another country that gives online shoppers a right to cancel (usually within 14 days of delivery), that right still applies: email us within that period to say you’re cancelling, then send the item back unworn and with its tags within 14 days. We refund the item price and the standard shipping charge within 14 days of receiving it back. Return postage is paid by you.

Refunds go back to the original payment method. Your bank may take a few days to show them.

==================== SHIPPING POLICY ====================

We ship worldwide.

Pre-orders
Pre-order pieces ship by the ship-by date shown on the product page, in your bag and on your order confirmation. If an order is going to be later than that, we’ll email you before the date with a new one, and you can choose a full refund instead of waiting.

Rates
United States: $8, or free on orders of $200 or more.
Everywhere else: $35.
The exact shipping cost for your address is shown at checkout before you pay.

Delivery
Delivery times depend on the carrier and on customs in your country, so any estimate is a guide rather than a guarantee. When your order ships, we email you the carrier and a tracking link where one is available.

Customs and duties
International orders may be charged import duties and taxes by the destination country. These aren’t included in our prices or shipping, and are paid by you to the carrier or customs on delivery.

Your address
Please check your shipping address at checkout. We can’t redirect a parcel once it has left us, and a parcel returned to us as undeliverable is re-sent at your cost.

Damaged or incorrect items
If your order arrives damaged or isn’t what you ordered, email us at neverhollowonline@gmail.com as soon as you can, with your order number and photos, and we’ll put it right.
